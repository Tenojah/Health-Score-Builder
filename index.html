The project

Weight your health indicators, spot the accounts at risk, and launch the right playbook before renewal.

How does it work?

1. You set the weights

Pick what matters for your business: usage, adoption, NPS, tickets, sponsor engagement.

2. The score ranks your accounts

Red, amber and green status, with the main factor pulling each score down.

3. You launch the playbook

Each at-risk account gets a suggested first action, ready to act on.

How to use the tool (30 seconds)
  1. Set the weights. Raise the indicators that matter most for your business: product usage for a consumption-based SaaS, executive sponsor engagement for large annual contracts.
  2. Set your thresholds. By default a score below 60 is red and 80 or above is green. Adapt them to your portfolio.
  3. Read the table. At-risk accounts come first. The main factor shows what pulls the score down, and the playbook suggests the first action to launch.
  4. Import your accounts. Download the CSV template, fill in account, arr and the 5 scores from 0 to 100 (usage, adoption, nps, tickets, sponsor), then import it. Your data stays in your browser.
